# Chibi Anime Animation for Seedance 2.0 on Higgsfield

## Understanding Chibi as a Visual Style

Chibi (ちび) — literally "small" in Japanese — is a deliberate stylistic choice where characters are drawn with exaggerated proportions: oversized heads (2-3 head-to-body ratio instead of the normal 7-8), enormous sparkling eyes, tiny bodies, and stubby limbs. It's not simplified animation — it's a specific aesthetic of extreme cuteness (kawaii) that conveys emotion through exaggeration.

Chibi is used in anime for comedic moments, merchandise, fan art, and entire shows (*Nendoroid* style, *Isekai Quartet*, *Attack on Titan: Junior High*). The style reduces complex characters to their most recognizable visual elements while maximizing emotional expressiveness through oversized facial features.

---

## Core Aesthetic Principles

### Visual Signatures
- **2:1 or 3:1 head-to-body ratio** — enormous heads on tiny bodies
- **Oversized sparkling eyes** — filling most of the face, with multiple highlight reflections
- **Minimal nose and mouth** — tiny dot nose, simple curved mouth
- **Stubby limbs** — short arms and legs, often without visible fingers
- **Round, soft shapes** — no sharp angles on characters
- **Vibrant pastel palette** — candy colors, soft pinks, lavenders, mint greens
- **Sparkle/star effects** — constant sparkles, hearts, and stars around characters

### Color Philosophy
- **Candy-colored palette** — bright pastels: pink, lavender, mint, peach, sky blue, lemon
- **White/cream backgrounds** — clean, bright, no dark or moody tones
- **Blush marks** — pink circles on cheeks, always present
- **Sparkle highlights** — white star-shaped highlights in eyes and on surfaces
- **No dark shadows** — minimal shading, what exists is soft and pastel

### Animation Characteristics
- **Bouncy movement** — characters bounce, hop, and wiggle constantly
- **Exaggerated expressions** — enormous tears, anger veins, sweat drops, heart eyes
- **Simple backgrounds** — pastel gradients, pattern fills, or candy-themed environments
- **Sparkle particles** — constant floating sparkles, hearts, stars, musical notes
- **Squash and stretch** — extreme, rubbery deformation for comedic effect

## 2-Second Hook Framework

1. **Sparkle Explosion** — Screen fills with sparkles, chibi character pops into frame
2. **Giant Eye Blink** — Extreme close-up of oversized sparkling eyes blinking
3. **Bounce Entrance** — Character bounces into frame from above, lands with a cute wobble
4. **Heart Burst** — Hearts explode outward from center, revealing the chibi character
5. **Chibi Transform** — Normal character shrinks into chibi form with a *poof* effect
